I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Global Research Center for Quantum Information Science](https://quantum.nii.ac.jp) hosted in [National Institute of Informatics](https://www.nii.ac.jp/en/), Tokyo, Japan. 

My work lies at the interface of quantum information and analytical description of interacting quantum systems, with a focus on scalable methods for quantum technologies.

Current research interests
----

- **Numerical simulation of quantum platforms:**  
  I develop analytical techniques to enable scalable numerical simulations of light–matter Hamiltonians, with particular emphasis on waveguide QED platforms.

- **Quantum benchmarking & certification:**  
  I work on scalable protocols for the certification of quantum operations, with a particular focus on randomized benchmarking approaches for distributed gates (e.g., remote CNOT).


Previous research
----
During my PhD, I worked on quantum algorithms for Bayesian inference, with applications to signal detection and resource allocation in communication networks (e.g., 5G).

- I explored Hamiltonian formulations of inference problems.
- I designed execution strategies based on averaging over hyperparameters to reduce algorithmic overhead.
- I studied how to mitigate connectivity constraints in quantum annealers, given the hardware limitations at the time.
